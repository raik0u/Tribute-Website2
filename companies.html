The Bay Area is home to many famous companies that have shaped the technology industry and beyond. From Silicon Valley giants to innovative startups, the region continues to be a hub for creativity and innovation.

Silicon Valley Giants

Silicon Valley is home to some of the world's most influential technology companies, often known as the “Silicon Valley giants.” Companies such as Apple, Google, Meta, Salesforce, and Tesla have helped shape modern technology through innovations in smartphones, artificial intelligence, social media, software, electric vehicles, and online services. Located in the San Francisco Bay Area, Silicon Valley has become a global center for technology, entrepreneurship, and innovation.
